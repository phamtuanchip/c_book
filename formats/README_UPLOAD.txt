Upload instructions:
1. Sign in to KDP (https://kdp.amazon.com) and click 'Create a new Kindle eBook'.
2. Enter book details using metadata in kdp/metadata.yaml (language: vi, author: Lukas).
3. In 'Kindle eBook Content' upload formats/book.epub as 'Manuscript'.
4. Upload formats/cover.jpg as 'Book cover' (or let KDP create a cover from manuscript).
5. Set pricing, territories, and publish.

Notes:
- Fonts embedded: NotoSans-Regular.ttf, NotoSans-Bold.ttf (open-source). Safe to distribute.
- Ensure book preview looks correct in Kindle Previewer or on KDP.
